What it is

A batch-level stock manager for pharmacies: log every batch as it arrives with its expiry date, dispense against automatic first-expiry-first-out (FEFO) logic, and get an accurate in-date stock count for any medicine at any moment.

Who it's for

Neighbourhood and independent pharmacies currently tracking batches on paper or in a spreadsheet, where it's easy to accidentally dispense an older batch after a newer one, or lose track of what's about to expire on the shelf.

How it helps

No more manually checking dates before handing over a strip of tablets, and no more discovering expired stock only when a customer complains. The pharmacist logs in, sees exactly what's sellable and what's expiring, and dispenses with one click knowing the oldest-safe batch was used.

What we'd build next

  • Supplier reorder suggestions — auto-flag medicines whose in-date stock has dropped below a threshold.
  • Barcode scanning — scan a batch on arrival and at dispense time instead of typing batch codes by hand.
  • Multi-branch view — for pharmacies with more than one location, a combined stock and alerts dashboard.
